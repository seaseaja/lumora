How to apply your directions

  1. Find true directions. Use a phone compass app away from metal and electronics. Stand in the middle of the room, or at your desk, and note where North is.
  2. Desk & work: sit so that you face your +1 direction (or +4 for study). Your face points the way, not your back.
  3. Bed: place the bed so the top of your head points toward your +2 direction (or +3 for couples) while you sleep.
  4. Front door: a main door that faces one of your supportive directions is considered favourable.
  5. If the ideal is not possible, use any supportive direction in order +1 → +4. Any supportive direction is better than an unfavourable one, and a comfortable, practical layout matters more than a perfect angle.

Method: the Eight Mansions (Ba Zhai) system derives a personal Kua number from the solar birth year and the selected traditional formula, then maps it to four supportive and four unfavourable compass directions. Each direction covers a 45° sector. This method does not calculate auspicious calendar days or evaluate a whole home's layout, and it is a traditional practice for reflection, not a guarantee of results.
