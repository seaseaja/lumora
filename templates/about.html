ABOUT LUMORA

A little clarity, your way.

Lumora is a reflection tool. It brings chat analysis, tarot, the Destiny Matrix, and Zodiac & Shio together in one place so you can look at your relationships and choices from a few different angles.

What you can do

Chat Analysis

Upload a WhatsApp export to see a closeness score from 0 to 100, how it changes month by month, and communication patterns based on Gottman's research. The analysis follows fixed rules and keyword lists. It is not a trained AI model, and every report explains how its score was calculated.

Tarot

Ask a question, draw cards, and read an interpretation. With your consent, AI can suggest a spread, write the reading, and answer follow-up questions. Without AI, readings use the card meanings in Lumora's own card data.

Destiny Matrix

Calculate your matrix from your birth date and explore what each position describes.

Zodiac & Shio

See your Western sign, your Chinese zodiac animal and element, and your Kua number with its favourable directions. Lumora uses the Lunar New Year date, not 1 January, to work out your Shio.

Your privacy

  • Chat messages are only read while your upload is being processed. Lumora saves the numbers and aliases from the analysis, not the messages themselves.
  • Tarot questions and card names are only sent to the AI provider if you tick the consent box, and the provider is asked not to store them.
  • Your readings stay in your history so you can open them again, and only you can see them.

A gentle note

Lumora's readings are for reflection and fun. They are not predictions, and they are not professional advice on psychology, health, money, or law. A closeness score or a tarot card can't tell you everything about a person or a relationship. If something important is on your mind, talk to someone you trust or to a qualified professional.
